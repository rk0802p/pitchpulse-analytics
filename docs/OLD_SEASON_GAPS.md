# Historical season gaps

Phase 2 is restricted to 2025/26. The independent cross-check found pre-existing coverage gaps in 2014/15 (including incomplete Burnley, Atlético Madrid and Villarreal records) and 2020/21 (including incomplete Real Madrid attacking-player coverage). These gaps were investigated only and were not repaired or regenerated.

No historical season outside 2025/26 was changed by Phase 2.
